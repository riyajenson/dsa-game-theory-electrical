        FIELD MANUAL / MULTIPLAYER

        Make every decision count

        Every Transmit sends one packet along the C++ energy aware route to terminal 05. Each intermediate sensor must choose Relay that round. A refusal or empty route loses the packet. Low energy and low trust can change the route. Relay forwards all packets crossing your sensor, earning 12 points per packet plus 4 for a helpful round. An own delivery earns 20 points. Final score also counts trust and remaining battery, and subtracts 5 per Sleep. Sleep can keep a sensor alive for later rounds, while Relay can open a teammate’s route.
