# Phase 5 Completion Checklist

## Completed

- Node-state cards show node identifiers, energy bars, reputation, strategy, and visible suspicious-node highlighting.
- Route cards show the shortest and energy-aware paths with their costs.
- Metric cards present the deterministic Phase 4 simulation metrics.
- Strategy comparison bars summarize node counts and average energy by strategy.
- The responsive layout remains usable on narrow screens, and dynamic content includes accessible headings, labels, live regions, and energy progress-bar semantics.
- The dashboard is dependency-free and scoped to local static use.
